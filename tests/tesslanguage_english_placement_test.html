Discover your current English level and get a personalised recommendation for your next stage of learning.

  • Grammar and vocabulary

Before you begin: choose the best answer for each question. Try not to use dictionaries or translators. Listening recordings are built into the test — headphones are recommended.

How it works

Questions gradually increase in difficulty. Your result combines your section scores and performance on level-tagged questions; it is an estimate, not an official certificate.

You can skip optional writing, but complete all objective sections for the most useful result.
